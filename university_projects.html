Capstone Project

My capstone project represents the pinnacle of my undergraduate education, combining theoretical knowledge with practical engineering skills. Working with a team of six students, we developed a hydraulic cylinder test bench for industrial applications, focusing on automation, data collection, and report generation.

  • Hydraulic Cylinder Test Bench - Complete system design and implementation
  • Electrical control system design and programming
  • Python-based automated report generation system
