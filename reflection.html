My Learning Journey

Throughout the Capstone course and this project, I haven't learned much about the design process. The course reinforced what I already knew about designing a product. However, I learned the importance of acquiring new skills to achieve results. I spent significant time learning necessary skills, as we had to adjust our direction and deliverables based on client communication.

Based on my experiences in this course, the most important attributes of an engineer are integrity, determination, tenacity, conscientiousness, and a strong focus on safety. These qualities ensure that engineers can effectively solve problems, adapt to changing circumstances, and maintain high standards of professionalism and responsibility in their work.

My experience on this project has deepened my understanding of the role and responsibilities of a professional engineer in society. Engineers must provide effective solutions to their clients while ensuring these solutions are safe for everyone potentially affected. This responsibility extends beyond the client to the broader community, emphasizing the importance of safety, ethical considerations, and the societal impact of engineering decisions.

I have learned a lot about how to approach problems, and I have developed the tenacity and determination to find ways to solve each problem thrown my way. As for areas where I need to grow, I lack on-the-job experience. To address this, I plan to find an EIT position to gain practical experience. This will help me apply my academic knowledge in real-world scenarios, further develop my skills, and better prepare me for a successful engineering career.
