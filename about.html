Bio

I am an electrical engineering graduate from the University of British Columbia, based in Calgary, Alberta. I completed my Bachelor of Applied Science in 2025, with interests in circuit design, semiconductor devices, and microfabrication.

Since March 2026, I have worked for Calgary Sports and Entertainment as the Calgary Flames Breakfast Chef. I prepare meals for the Flames and also support the Calgary Stampeders, Calgary Wranglers, and other events. The role involves early starts, independent work, and adapting to changing schedules while maintaining consistent food quality.

Alongside my work, I continue to develop my knowledge of integrated circuit design and fabrication through independent study and practical projects. My current work uses Electric VLSI and LTspice to build schematics and simulate CMOS circuits. This website documents my projects, technical learning, and reading interests.

Projects

My projects include university coursework, my engineering capstone, and independent circuit design studies. They document my experience with circuit analysis, simulation, layout, and Python programming.

  • Personal Projects: Independent work in circuit design, simulation, and semiconductor layout.
  • Capstone Project: A hydraulic cylinder test bench, including electrical control system design and Python-based automated report generation.
  • University Projects: Selected work from my electrical engineering degree, including MEMS thermal annealing and PPG sensor design.
