Circuit Design and Simulation

In this project I am transitioning from the theoretical knowledge found in the textbooks I am reading to the practical application of it, through circuit design and simulation using LTSpice, and layout design using KLayout.

Overview

The purpose of this project is to build and develop skills that will aid me in transitioning from a student to a professional in the field of semiconductor design. I will be utilizing the end of chapter questions in Analog Integrated Circuit Design by Tony C. Causone, David A. Johns, and Kennth W. Martin as a source of practice problems to solve. The goal is to work through each problem, then to simulate the designed circuit in LTSpice, and finally create a layout for the circuit in KLayout. This is an ongoing project.

Technical Details

  • Circuit simulation in LTSpice
  • Layout design in KLayout
  • Develop and practice LVS and DRC checks

Outcomes and Learning

This project is still in its infancy. I am currently developing the DRC files for the different CMOS processes found in the textbook.
